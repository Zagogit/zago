About me

Developer, trainer and entrepreneur

I'm Salisu Idris Zago, a software developer and digital skills coach based in Kano. I design and build websites, mobile apps and custom software for individuals, businesses and organisations that want a reliable online presence.

Alongside development, I train people in artificial intelligence, content generation and social media management, so they can use technology to grow their work, brands and income.

I am the founder of Zago Media Project, Dynamic Informatics Academy, and Media Consults and Software Solutions. Whether you want a product built or a skill learned, I focus on clear communication, practical results and work you can be proud to show.
